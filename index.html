Do you help individuals and businesses?

Yes. CrumByte focuses on computer and systems repair for individuals, and IT consulting, infrastructure and managed-service conversations for businesses.

Can you troubleshoot my computer remotely?

Many software, configuration and connectivity issues can be assessed remotely. Hardware failures usually require physical inspection. We'll explain the options for your situation.

Do you repair laptops and desktop PCs?

We accept inquiries for laptops and desktop computers, including hardware diagnostics, upgrades, operating system concerns and performance issues. Repair feasibility depends on the device and issue.

How much does an IT service cost?

Pricing depends on the device, issue, scope and required parts or services. Contact us with the details so we can discuss an appropriate estimate.

Do you offer ongoing managed IT services?

Businesses can inquire about ongoing support, maintenance planning, remote assistance and managed IT arrangements tailored to their needs.
