WHO WE ARE

Technology support with a human approach.

CrumByte Technologies is a Hawaii-based IT services business built around a simple idea: good technology support should be both capable and approachable.

We work with businesses that need help keeping systems dependable, solving difficult problems, and making informed technology decisions. That can mean managing everyday IT needs, reviewing a network, maintaining servers, or helping plan what comes next.

Rather than recommend complexity for its own sake, we start with your priorities, existing tools, and the outcomes that matter to your organization.
